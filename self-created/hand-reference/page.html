The seven poses

Each already exists in the game as an animated gesture. A photograph of the same pose is what lets me correct it — the numbers came from motion reference, and every one is a guess until a real hand sits next to it.

Holdwhile the mic is open

Both hands raised toward the middle of your view, wrists close, fingers up and spread, palms turned away. The pose held while you speak.

Thrustfrost lance · storm lance · nova beam

Three frames: wound back as a loose fist, driving forward with the leading hand open, and the settle just after.

Overheadcinder fall · solar phoenix

Hands raised high, then coming down through the shot. The top of the raise and the bottom of the slam.

Groundvoltaic snare · glacial crown · gravity singularity · worldroot bloom

Both palms turned down and pressed away from you, fingers fanned.

Sweeprift sever

One arm cutting diagonally across your body while the other braces. The start and the end of the cut.

Shoveforce repulse

Both palms out flat, pushing rather than throwing.

Gatherverdant heal

Hands drawing inward toward your chest — the one gesture that comes back rather than going out.

How to shoot them

From your own eyeline. Phone at eye height, pointing where you would be looking. Not a photograph of your hands from in front — a photograph of what you see.

Both hands in frame. Even when one leads, the other is in shot and doing something. That relationship is most of what makes it read.

Plain ground, even light. A wall, a floor, daylight. Hard shadows hide the finger separation, which is the thing being measured.

Hold each pose still. Motion blur is fine for feeling and useless for geometry. One sharp frame per pose beats a burst.

Name them if you can. A filename like thrust-back saves me guessing which of the seven it is.
